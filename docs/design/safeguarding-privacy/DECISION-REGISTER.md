# Human decision register

No agent may infer these choices from repository access, a learner's age, a guardian label, technical capability, or desire for better analytics. Until each applicable row has an identified accountable human, evidence, jurisdiction/deployment scope, approval date/version, and review trigger, the boundary is **synthetic/local/default deny**.

| ID | Required decision | Safe default / unblock evidence |
|---|---|---|
| H-001 | deployment countries/regions, service audience, controller/operator roles, and applicable legal review | no production learner processing; written scoped review by accountable operator/counsel |
| H-002 | age/independent-consent thresholds, guardian verification, educator delegation, assent, and conflicting-role process | unknown/pending; reviewed relationship policy and verification service/process |
| H-003 | exact real-data inventory and purpose per field, including whether raw conversation is ever essential | structured synthetic fixtures only; approved data-protection/product specification proving necessity |
| H-004 | retention events/durations, backup deletion, correction/export identity checks, legal hold, and account/role termination | no real records; reviewed schedule and tested rights workflow |
| H-005 | application encryption, key custody/recovery/rotation, administrator access, device loss, and audit access | no restricted storage; reviewed security architecture and recovery test |
| H-006 | external AI/cloud/sync providers, region/subprocessors, payload, logging/training, retention/deletion, incidents, and fallback | no learner payload; approved provider assessment/config/contract and payload audit |

| H-010 | secondary research, model training, commercial use, advertising, public sharing, and cross-curriculum evidence import | prohibited; separate explicit purpose, authority, ethics/privacy review, and opt-out/deletion contract |

## #5 blocker B-001: narrowed boundary

The architecture resolves what can proceed: #5 may design and test, using synthetic data only, a privacy-minimized structured feedback schema, local analyzer, disclosure-risk checks, synthetic issue rendering, exact-payload confirmation, and non-sensitive linkage/audit. It may measure pipeline correctness and simulated learning-velocity calculations without claims about real learners.

B-001 remains open for **all real transcript or learner-derived collection, retention, analysis, provider transfer, aggregate disclosure, and public issue generation** until at minimum H-001–H-008 are scoped and approved as applicable. A blanket “consent” is insufficient: the answer must bind purpose, fields, population/relationship, access, provider, retention/deletion/export, incident handling, disclosure threshold, and external-write reviewer. Approval for structured learning records does not imply approval for raw conversation.

## Reassessment triggers

Re-review on new population/jurisdiction, provider, data field/class, purpose, export/share route, authority role, safeguarding signal, accessibility failure, security/privacy incident, model-training behavior, retention change, or material product architecture change. Existing authority never silently expands to the new scope.
