# Flow controller

The controller seeks productive challenge: clear goal, focused action, usable feedback, learner control, and difficulty near current capability. Flow is uncertain and mostly diagnostic; delayed mastery and well-being remain the success criteria.

## Observable inputs

- target-specific correctness and misconception sequence;
- scaffold/help trajectory and whether help changes the next attempt;
- response-latency buckets and interruption/reorientation cost;
- confidence/challenge reports when voluntarily supplied;
- repeated skips, pause/stop, accessibility friction, and fallback use;
- immediate versus prior delayed evidence.

Do not infer emotion, disability, or engagement from camera, voice affect, biometrics, identity, or covert profiling.

## Policy table

| Pattern | Cautious hypothesis | Change one variable | Confirm with |
|---|---|---|---|
| repeated target errors + rising help | challenge or prerequisite gap | increase scaffold, reduce complexity, or switch to worked example | success on a near transfer, then fade |
| slow but improving, low help | productive effort | keep level; reduce irrelevant time pressure | next two attempts and learner check-in |
| fast accurate responses + low transfer | superficial familiarity | vary context or require production/explanation | novel unaided item |
| high immediate success + weak delayed recall | fluency illusion | schedule spacing/retrieval | delayed unaided attempt |
| random errors or sudden latency rise | fatigue, ambiguity, distraction, or access barrier | pause, clarify, offer modality/control, or stop | learner correction and resumed pattern |
| frustration/self-reported excessive challenge | mismatch | honor pause/stop; simplify or change task | explicit learner response, not persistence |

## Adaptation boundaries

The assistant may change cosmetic state and apply preauthorized accessibility controls. It requests changes to scaffold, complexity, representation, pace, choice width, task type, or content. The agent checks objective preservation, evidence validity, prior history, and stop/safety constraints before responding.

Difficulty decreases are not failure: find the highest level at which the learner can perform meaningful work with useful effort. Then fade support cautiously. Difficulty increases are justified by learning evidence, not boredom prediction or a desire to extend the session.

## Anti-compulsion rules

- Never hide stop, pause, elapsed time, or natural completion.
- Never use streak loss, variable rewards, social pressure, or shame to prevent leaving.
- Never lower evidence standards to preserve a success animation.
- Never optimize raw session time, clicks, completion, or self-reported flow as the primary KPI.
- End with a visible truthful success and a later retrieval plan when educationally sound.
